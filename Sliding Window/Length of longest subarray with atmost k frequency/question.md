Length of Longest Subarray With at Most K Frequency

## Problem Statement

Given an integer array `nums` and an integer `k`, find the length of the longest contiguous subarray such that the frequency of every element in the subarray is at most `k`.

A subarray is a contiguous non-empty sequence of elements within an array.

### Example

Input:
nums = [1,2,3,1,2,3,1,2]
k = 2

Output:
6

Explanation:
The longest good subarray is [1,2,3,1,2,3] since each element occurs at most 2 times.
